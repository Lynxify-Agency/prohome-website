Broadwalk South, London

Broadwalk South is a terraced house in London where lighting, curtains, heating, music, video and security run on one Control4 system. A custom-built projector lift keeps the bedroom wall clear.

Delivered in London by Deividas, ProHome's founder, and his UK team.

Location
London, United Kingdom
Type
Private house
Scale
Terraced house
Timescale
6 months
Systems
Control4, lighting, curtains, heating, audio and video, security, network

The brief

The client wanted to run the whole house from one app and bring as many systems together as possible. Working from home most days, they also needed to see and control everything easily from the desk, with the budget spent where it made a real difference.

The challenge

A fairly small terraced house, modernised throughout, had to hold lighting, curtains, heating, audio, video and security without cluttering the walls. Every system needed to answer to one app, one remote and the desk, and the bedroom needed a projector that disappears when it is not in use.

The solution, system by system

One Control4 system

Every system is integrated into Control4 and run from a wall touch screen, a handheld remote or the phone app. Alexa voice scenes handle the everyday routines.

Lighting control

Rako lighting control with scenes for each room, timed to follow sunrise and sunset through the year.

Motorised curtains

Silent Gliss curtain tracks, run from the same touch screen, remote and app as the rest of the house.

Heating control

Heatmiser Neo heating, set and adjusted room by room through Control4.

Audio and video

Control4 multi-room audio with Bowers & Wilkins speakers, and a WyreStorm 4×4 HDBaseT matrix for video distribution. In the bedroom, a custom-built projector lift keeps the wall clear.

Security and access

A Texecom alarm and access control for the side gate, in the same app as the lights and heating.

Network

Structured Cat6 cabling and Wi-Fi, built to carry every system in the house.
